

Torques
-------

Suspensión delantera

- tornillo del amortiguador: 45 Nm
- rotulas brazo-buje: 55 Nm
- tornillos casquillos de los brazos: 45 Nm
- soporte de la pinza de freno: 45 Nm
- bieletas de la estabilizadora: 45 Nm


Top wishbone - insert from the outside of both pivot eyes (from front of front eye, and rear of rear eye).
Lower wishbone - insert from the inside of both pivot eyes (from rear of front eye, and front of rear eye).



Setup
-----

Torques

- mesa de la rótula superior: 68 Nm (si es grado 10.9) aparece en la cabeza del tornillo
